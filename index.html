About Senator Wadada

Senator Ahmed Wadada Aliyu is a chartered Nigerian Politician and influential political figure representing Nasarawa West Senatorial District. With a career spanning over two decades, he has earned recognition for his strategic leadership, strong grassroots connection, and commitment to public accountability.

He previously served two terms in the House of Representatives, where he chaired the House Committee on Capital Market and Institutions, Information and National Orientation. Beyond parliamentary duties, Senator Wadada has demonstrated exceptional capacity in political organization and institution-building.

Currently, as Chairman of the Senate Committee on Public Accounts, Senator Wadada plays a pivotal oversight role in promoting transparency, fiscal discipline, and accountability in government institutions. Renowned for his people-oriented approach, he continues to champion initiatives in youth empowerment, education, infrastructure, and economic development across Nasarawa State.
